Signature Trail

Devil's Punchbowl

The most dramatic spot on the central Oregon Coast. Walk down from the cliffs and stand at the edge of a natural rock cauldron where waves crash up to 50 feet into the air.

Right next to Flying Dutchman Winery — so you can hike down, watch the spectacle, hunt for agates on the surrounding beach, then walk up for a tasting. Agates and sea glass wash up on the beach around the Punchbowl after every storm.

Walking distance from the property — through the forest along the cliff trail

Before You Go

Trail tips from the front desk.

A few things to know before you head out — so every hike is safe and enjoyable.

🌊

Check the Tides

Tide pools and Gull Rock are best at low tide. Ask the front desk for a tide chart or check online before you go. The Punchbowl is spectacular at high tide.

🧥

Layer Up

Coastal weather changes fast. Bring a windbreaker even on sunny days — the bluff gets hit by ocean wind. Mornings are foggy, afternoons often clear.

👢

Sturdy Shoes

Trails can be muddy, especially in the forest sections. The bluff edges are uneven volcanic rock — closed-toe shoes or hiking boots are recommended.

🫐

Free Blackberries

Wild blackberries grow on the property — pick them on your walk. Peak season is late summer. Grab a container from the full kitchen before you head out.

🍄

Mushroom Season

Autumn (October–December) is prime mushroom foraging season on the 38-acre property. Chanterelles love the old-growth forest floors here.

🦦

Wildlife Watching

Watch for otters in the rocks along the cliff trail. Whales pass by December through June — you can spot spouts right from the hiking paths.
