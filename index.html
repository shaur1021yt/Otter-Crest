The Inn at Otter Crest sits on a 38-acre oceanfront property in Otter Rock, Oregon — a place that feels like it was discovered, not built. Built in 1970, the property spreads across forested bluff above the tide pools, where the Pacific meets volcanic rock.

Check in at the office and a 4-person transport brings you up to the suite. The rooms are set apart from the world: you sit in bed and watch whales spout on the horizon. The bed is set tall so you can see the ocean — there's a ladder for anyone who needs it. The living room has a fireplace and a queen fold-out bed behind a pull door, so the space divides for extra guests.

This isn't a cookie-cutter hotel. It's the Oregon Coast the way it used to be — wild blackberries you pick on the path, agates on the beach, and a tree covered in old fishing net floats marking the road toward the winery down the cliff trail.

Experiences

More than a room — a launchpad.

From volcanic geology to world-class wine, everything is within reach of your suite.

Hiking

Devil's Punchbowl

A unique spot right next to the winery. Walk down from the cliffs and watch waves crash up to 50 feet in this dramatic natural cauldron. Agates wash up on the surrounding beach.

Wine

Flying Dutchman Winery

Walk along the cliffs through the forest to reach this winery. Pass the net-float tree on the way and watch for otters in the rocks below. Tasting room steps from Devil's Punchbowl.

Geology

Gull Rock & Volcanic Rings

The island on the left side of the deck is Gull Rock. When the tide goes out, you can see lava rings from an ancient volcanic eruption — the evidence of a sonic boom etched into stone.

Local

Fresh Fish Harbor

Just 4 miles from the property, the fishing harbor sells the day's catch. Grab fresh Dungeness crab or rockfish and bring it back to your full kitchen to cook.
